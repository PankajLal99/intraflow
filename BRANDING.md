IntraFlow is a multi-tenant SaaS platform for WhatsApp Business automation. Build custom conversation flows visually, deploy AI agents, and manage customer interactions—all without code. Perfect for agencies, enterprises, and businesses who want to automate WhatsApp at scale.

### Long Description (150 words)
IntraFlow revolutionizes WhatsApp Business automation by combining visual workflow builders with AI-powered agents. Built for agencies, enterprises, and SaaS operators, IntraFlow enables you to create a multi-tenant platform where each customer can design their own conversation flows, connect their WhatsApp numbers, and automate customer interactions.

With IntraFlow, you get a complete solution: visual workflow builder (drag-and-drop), AI agents powered by LangChain, parent agent guardrails for safety, real-time analytics, team management, and billing integration. Deploy on your own infrastructure with Docker, scale to thousands of customers, and maintain 85-99% profit margins.

Built on open-source technologies (OpenWA, LangChain, PostgreSQL), IntraFlow gives you full control, no vendor lock-in, and the ability to customize every aspect. Start with a single EC2 instance for $70/month and scale as you grow.

- 🏢 **Multi-Tenant SaaS** - Unlimited customers, isolated data

- 📊 **Real-Time Analytics** - Monitor performance and user engagement
- 👥 **Team Management** - Roles, permissions, collaboration
- 💳 **Billing Ready** - Stripe integration, usage tracking
- 🐳 **Docker Deploy** - One-command deployment
- 📈 **Auto-Scaling** - From 1 to 10,000+ customers
- 🔓 **Open Source Core** - MIT licensed, no lock-in

## Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice
- **Professional** but approachable
- **Technical** but not jargon-heavy
- **Confident** but not arrogant
- **Helpful** and solution-oriented

### Writing Guidelines

**Do:**
- ✅ Use clear, concise language
- ✅ Explain technical concepts simply
- ✅ Focus on benefits, not just features
- ✅ Use active voice
- ✅ Be specific with numbers and metrics

**Don't:**
- ❌ Use buzzwords without substance
- ❌ Over-promise features
- ❌ Use jargon without explanation
- ❌ Make unverified claims
- ❌ Criticize competitors directly

### Example Copy

**Good:**
"IntraFlow helps you automate WhatsApp conversations with AI agents. Build flows visually, deploy in minutes, scale to thousands of customers."

**Bad:**
"IntraFlow leverages cutting-edge AI paradigms to revolutionize conversational experiences at enterprise scale."

## Competitive Positioning

### vs Twilio
- ✅ **90% cheaper** ($29 vs $150+/month)
- ✅ **Self-hosted** (full control)
- ✅ **Visual builder** (Twilio doesn't have one)
- ❌ Not officially Meta-certified (trade-off)

### vs ManyChat
- ✅ **Multi-tenant** (sell to customers)
- ✅ **Self-hosted** (your infrastructure)
- ✅ **AI agents** (ManyChat is rule-based)
- ✅ **Open source** (customize everything)

### vs Zapier
- ✅ **WhatsApp-first** (purpose-built)
- ✅ **Visual flow builder** (specifically for conversations)
- ✅ **Multi-tenant** (SaaS platform)
- ❌ Not as many integrations (yet)
